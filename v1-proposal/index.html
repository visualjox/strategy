The opportunity

The customer exists.
Nobody is speaking to them.

90% of India's potential gamers do not own a gaming PC. They want to play CS:GO, Valorant, GTA. They own a MacBook, an iPad, a low-spec laptop. The hardware barrier has stopped them — not the desire.

No platform in India is talking to this person specifically. GeForce Now requires existing hardware. Xbox Cloud Gaming is not optimised for the Indian market. The frustrated potential gamer — 18 to 28, college-educated, device-owning but hardware-limited — is completely unaddressed.

"Play any game. On any device. Without buying a gaming PC." — This is the one line that drives everything. It answers the customer's objection before they raise it.

The target customer

Not the hardcore
gamer.

The hardcore gamer already owns a Rs.80,000 gaming PC. They are not your customer.

Your customer is the frustrated potential gamer. 18 to 28 years old. Owns a MacBook, iPad or low-spec Windows laptop. Has looked at gaming PCs and decided Rs.60,000 to Rs.1,20,000 is not worth it for a hobby. Has given up — not because they do not want to game, but because the hardware barrier felt insurmountable.

This person exists in massive numbers across Mumbai, Pune and Bangalore. They will pay Rs.60 per hour to play the game they actually want — on the device they already own.

The approach

Organic first.
Always.

No paid ads in Month 1. The message is not proven yet. Spending on ads before organic validates the creative is burning money to find out what does not work.

The best performing content in this category will always be: device + game title + it actually works. Everything else supports this. Nothing replaces it.

Instagram Reels is the primary platform. YouTube Shorts is the distribution amplifier — every reel gets repurposed as a Short at zero extra effort. Reddit seeds the community. WhatsApp retains and converts trial users to paying users.

The 90-day plan

Three months.
Three jobs.

Month One
Prove it works
  • 20 reels published
  • Device test content series
  • 100 community DMs
  • 50 to 100 genuine followers
  • 20 to 30 trial users
Month Two
Build social proof
  • User generated content
  • WhatsApp community launched
  • Email capture live
  • 200 to 500 followers
  • First repeat paying users
Month Three
Own a game title
  • Weekly tournaments
  • Referral programme live
  • 500+ followers
  • 50 to 100 paying users
  • Word of mouth starting
